What it is

50 to 60 guests. 30 speakers. One hotel.

2 to 3 days at one of the best hotels in the market, in Aspen, Park City or similar. Roughly 50 to 60 paying guests and around 30 flown in speakers. Every meal, session and evening is part of the programme.

This is the endpoint the other formats build toward: the investor summit that stands alone as ours. The Gstaad benchmark shows the model works, with tickets at around 6,500 CHF. We run it with professional curation, a stronger roster, speakers treated properly, and institutional backing.

High profile, high end. Quality over quantity.

Who is in the room

Three groups, curated one by one.

I

Sophisticated capital that writes cheques

Family office principals, HNW individuals and senior allocators, drawn from the database built through the SuperReturn and A4M programmes. People who put money where their mouth is.

II

Dedicated longevity GPs and crossover investors

The specialist core, meeting their future LPs and co-investors.

III

A world class speaker roster

Top geroscientists and biotech founders, flown in and hosted at the 5 star venue, unpaid but treated impeccably. A US location makes this roster far easier to land than Europe.

Commercial model

Profitable at Gstaad scale, and it grows from there.

Premium tickets

Sponsorship

Wealth managers, private banks and longevity companies, each buying genuine presence in the room: dinner hosting, experience sponsorship, named sessions.

Around 5 partners, per the Gstaad benchmark

Membership

The event as the annual anchor of a members cohort, converting one off attendance into recurring community revenue over time.
